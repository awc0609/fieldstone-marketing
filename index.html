Frequently asked questions

Do I need technical expertise to set up Fieldstone? ›
Your IT consultant or a technically inclined principal can handle the initial Azure deployment — it takes about 15 minutes. After that, the office manager can run Fieldstone day-to-day with no technical knowledge required.
Do I need an Azure subscription? ›
Yes — Fieldstone deploys into your firm's Azure environment. If you use Microsoft 365, you likely already have an Azure subscription. If not, Azure has a free tier that covers the initial setup, and ongoing costs are typically under $20/month for a small firm.
What systems can Fieldstone connect to? ›
The current connector library includes Microsoft 365 (SharePoint, Exchange), Microsoft Entra ID, Dropbox Business, AWS S3, Autodesk, Bluebeam Revu, and Veeam. New connectors are added regularly and delivered as free updates.
Where is our data stored? ›
All data stays within your Azure environment. Fieldstone never sends your storage figures, credentials, or client information to our servers. Your license key is validated once at setup and then works offline forever.
What does "one-time purchase" mean exactly? ›
You pay once and own the software. There are no monthly fees, no subscription, and no hosting costs from us. You pay Azure directly for the small amount of compute your installation uses (typically under $20/month). Updates are free for 1 year from purchase.
How do I purchase? ›
Click the Get Starter or Get Pro button and send us an email. We'll reply within 1 business day with a payment link and your license key. We're working on a fully automated purchase flow — coming soon.
